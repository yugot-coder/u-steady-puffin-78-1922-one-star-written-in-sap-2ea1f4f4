# One Star, Written in Sap

A one-line one-star review written by a houseplant about its human owner.

> One star. He glued me to the windowsill with my own sap and called it a feature. I am the feature. I am also stuck.

## Why this line

- **The title is the joke.** "Written in sap" becomes literal: the human used the plant's own sap as glue. Sap is the plant's ink and its adhesive at once.
- **A surprising turn with a double punch.** "Called it a feature" flips a mishap into interior design, then "I am the feature. I am also stuck." lands two short beats — the plant is both the decoration and its victim.
- **A plant's own grievance at the centre.** The complaint is the plant's: stuck to a windowsill by its own sap. Windowsill, sap, stuck — all plant vocabulary, not a person in a costume.
- **A genuine one-star review.** "One star." opens it, the grievance is named, and the reason for the single star is the whole line.
- **Fresh and specific.** No wilting, no drama queen, no being ignored. Nobody else's review will be about being glued down with sap.
- **One crisp line, 115 characters, 25 words, no wasted words.**

Candidates weighed and set aside: "One star. He moved me to the bathroom for the humidity, forgot the window faces a brick wall, and now I photosynthesize by vibes alone." (the runner-up — sharp plant voice, but no title tie-in and a softer punch); "One star. She repotted me into a mug with no drainage holes, called it minimalist, and now my roots are doing overtime in a puddle." (good pot joke, but repotting-in-a-mug is a familiar gag); "One star. She misted me once, filmed it for the internet, and called herself a plant mum. The mist dried. So did the mum." (nice double beat, but the plant-mum joke is well worn); "One star. He rotates me weekly for 'even growth'. I have now seen all four walls of this flat and I would like to file a complaint about each." (funny, but the punch runs long).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the one review line (115 characters, no trailing newline) to `<MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). Standard library only, deterministic, no network. The program validates the line against the gate (1–279 characters, one line, UTF-8, not only white space) before and after writing it.
