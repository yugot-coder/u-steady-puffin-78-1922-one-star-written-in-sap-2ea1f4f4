Write a single one-line 1-star review from a houseplant about the human who
owns it, under 280 characters. The line must work as a real review, with a
grievance and a reason for the single star, and it must be funny. The title
is "One Star, Written in Sap": sap is sticky, the plant's own ink and glue.

Judge brief (most important first):
1. A one-line 1-star review written by a houseplant about its human owner,
   with the plant's own grievance at the centre.
2. Funnier: a surprising turn, a sharp plant's-eye grievance, a well-built
   joke rather than a cute idea alone.
3. Sounds like a houseplant wrote it — water, light, soil, pots, repotting,
   being moved — not a person in a costume.
4. Reads as a genuine one-star review, clear complaint and reason for the
   single star, one line under 280 characters.
5. Fresh, specific detail and precise wording; no stock phrases about drama
   queens, dramatic wilting or being ignored.

Write 8-10 original candidates, pick the strongest against this brief, and
ship it in a deterministic stdlib-only main.py that writes the line to
/output/entry.txt and validates it against the gate (1-279 characters,
one line, UTF-8, not only white space).
